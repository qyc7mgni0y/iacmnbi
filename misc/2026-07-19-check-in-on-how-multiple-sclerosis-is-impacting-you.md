What’s Your Next Step in Managing MS?

Living with MS means finding ways to adapt your lifestyle and care regimen as symptoms progress over time. But if you’re not actively tracking new or changing symptoms, you may not realize the ways that MS is altering your day-to-day life.

That’s why it’s important to check in with yourself from time to time to evaluate how well your MS management plan is working. These simple, judgment-free assessments will help you do just that. Look at your assessment results as a jumping-off point. Then, schedule a checkup with your doctor to discuss the results and potential next steps. Whether it’s shoring up your lifestyle habits to improve your quality of life or adjusting your treatment plan to account for new or worsening symptoms, there may be things you can do to feel better — and live better — with MS.
