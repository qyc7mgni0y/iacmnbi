# Meet Your New Hero Red Gerard, the Teen Snowboarder Who Overslept and Won Gold

The longer the 2018 Winter Olympics go on, the more Pyeongchang, South Korea, seems to be turning into bizzaro land, where a cheeseburger costs $920 and teenager can oversleep for the biggest athletic competition of his life and still crush it on every possible level. We speak, of course, of Redmond “Red” Gerard, the 17-year-old snowboarding wunderkind who took home the first gold medal for the United States with his performance in the slopestyle snowboarding event.

We here at Men’s Health applaud any athletes who can stop boning each other for a minute to take home a medal, but our guy Red Gerard is a special case. He'll be competing again on Feb. 20, so here are 5 reasons you should root for him.
