# The Real Winner of the Oscars Is James McAvoy's Autographed Shirt

It’s not uncommon for Oscars after-parties to get a little raucous; celebs want to celebrate and commiserate after being on their best behavior during the ceremony. But for one A-list actor, last night’s Vanity Fair party resembled the last day of school more than a Hollywood hootenanny. Split and X-Men star James McAvoy emerged from the festivities with a shirt full of autographs.

[The Real](https://pepnex.cc/category/uxedv09qtx)

Competing for best dressed against Billy Porter’s black tie gown and Nicholas Hoult’s Dior tuxedo, McAvoy invited his famous friends to sign his shirt after appearing pristine on-stage to present the award for Best Sound Editing alongside Danai Gurira. He then took to Instagram to show off the Sharpie signatures, saying: “Went to the Oscars. Presented. Got mugged by sharpie wielding celebrities. #thuglebrity I’m sure my shirt is going to be defaced by more of these entitled graffiti mentalists as the night goes on.”

The shirt features the signatures of Black Panther star Michael B. Jordan, Best Actress nominee Glenn Close, and one fifth of Queer Eye’s Fab Five, Karamo Brown, alongside a few more we can’t quite make out.

[James McAvoy's](https://skin.peppysun.com/blog/3900423720.html)

McAvoy will reportedly put the shirt up for sale, with proceeds going to charity. He works with a number of them, including UNICEF and Ronald McDonald House (which is currently included in his Instagram bio). However, speaking to Entertainment Tonight, he said he’s currently unsure where the shirt will eventually be auctioned off, saying: “this was off the cuff and alcohol-fueled.”
