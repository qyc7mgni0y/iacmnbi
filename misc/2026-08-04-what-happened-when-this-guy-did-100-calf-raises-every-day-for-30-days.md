Fitness YouTuber Brett Maverick is on a mission: to make some serious calf gains.

"I am part of the Small Calves Club," he says to open his most recent challenge. "Yes, I have small calves, mainly due to my genetics. I used to work them really hard and pursue growing them, but it just didn't work out."

So, he's taking dramatic measures to try to kickstart growth: a 30-day challenge to see if he can make some "sweet gains in the calf department."

He measures his calves before he starts. Both his right and left leg muscles measure just under 15 inches in diameter.

After Day 1, he's already feeling it.

"I gotta say, my freakin' calves are on fire," he says. "I didn't expect it to burn that much, that was actually hard."

He measures his calves after the raises to see if the work made a difference, and they are slightly larger, at just over 15 inches. This immediate boost could be because of the blood still in his muscles just after exercise—the actual conditions behind the "pump" sensation bodybuilders chase in the gym. Maverick's biggest challenge, then, would be to stimulate actual growth.

He keeps going, and after Day 30, he does a final measurement. Both his calves clock in at 15.1 inches, just a slight growth.

"Maybe a 1/8 inch growth, not very much," says Maverick. "But we did see an increase in the appearance of my veins, which is exciting because I've never really had leg veins before."

He gives his final thoughts.

"This whole experiment kind of just confirms what I thought I already knew about calves: it's that they are extremely stubborn and mostly genetic, unfortunately," says Maverick.

Of course, growing your calves is not impossible, and requires more training.

"So how do you grow your calves? You have to lift extremely heavy and put more force on them than just the weight of your body. You've got to progressively overload that," he says.

Want to grow your calves? Like Maverick said, much of the size has to do with genetics—but if you train the two muscles that make up the calf, the gastrocnemius and the soleus, you might have more success. Try these exercises, along with calf raises, so you can build strength along with size.
