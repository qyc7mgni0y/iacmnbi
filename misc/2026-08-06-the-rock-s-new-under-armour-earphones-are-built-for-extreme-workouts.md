The best workouts come when you’re in the zone and listening to the best music. That’s why The Rock is back with a new pair of earphones, built to tune out the world and give you quality sound. The actor teamed up with Under Armour hoping to top his True Wireless earphones from last year with the new Project Rock True Wireless X by JBL, out today.

UA True Wireless Flash X Project Rock Edition Headphones

Designed with a sweat and waterproof material, the earbuds were built to handle extreme workouts in all conditions, and give you some quality sound in the process. Its Bluetooth 5.0 and fast pair feature means you can quickly and easily connect the earphones to your devices whether you’re pumping iron in the weight room or out running trails.

But don’t worry about having to constantly take your buds in and out of your ear. The “Bionic Hearing” allows users to stay locked in the zone during workouts with no distractions, while still maintaining awareness and the ability to hold conversations with a workout partner, thanks to its passive noise-cancellation features.

After upgrading the full and all-encompassing sound from its previous version, according to JBL, these new Rocked-out wireless earphones can now hold up to 50 hours of battery life, 10 hours of playtime with an extra 40 hours in the aluminum portable charging case. And the USB Type C charger speeds up your charging time so you can get back to the grind.
