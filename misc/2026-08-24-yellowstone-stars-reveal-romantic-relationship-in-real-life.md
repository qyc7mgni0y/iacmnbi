When it comes to a gritty, violent, sprawling Western family saga like Yellowstone, where loyalties are forever being tested and questioned and characters can meet brutal ends at the drop of a cowboy hat, you might think it better if life not imitate art. But one of the show's storylines—or at least, a far less stressful version—has made it off the screen and into the real world.

Ryan Bingham and Hassie Harrison, who play couple Walker and Laramie in the series, have just confirmed that they are dating in real life. Bingham shared a pic of the two of them kissing on Instagram, standing in front of a brushfire.

"More than a spark," Bingham wrote in the caption for the photo, tagging Harrison, who replied in the comments: "I love you, cowboy."
