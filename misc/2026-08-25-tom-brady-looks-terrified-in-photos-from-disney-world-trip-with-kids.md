# Tom Brady Looks Terrified in Photos From Disney World Trip With Kids

With a record-breaking seven Super Bowl wins to his name and a solid reputation as one of the greatest football players of all time across his tenure with both the Pats and the Bucs, Tom Brady was damn-near unstoppable during his time with the NFL. He has approached his post-retirement life with the same level of energy, including stripping down and showing off his lean physique to boost sales of his Brady Brand underwear.

But it turns out, there are some things in this world that do faze the former quarterback—like rollercoasters.

Brady just shared a series of photos to Instagram taken during a recent trip to Disney World in Orlando, Florida with his children, the first of which shows him on the Tower of Terror looking mightily freaked out while his daughter Vivian appears entirely unbothered.

"The happiest place on earth (until you realize your kids were lying about tower of terror being 'mad chill')," Brady joked in the caption.

Brady has been actively involved in co-parenting Vivan, 10, and Benjamin, 13, since his divorce from supermodel Gisele Bündchen in 2022, and even more so since retiring (for the second time) from pro football.

"Fatherhood is a great responsibility — being available and present is the most important thing," he recently told Today. "Something that I continue to work on is making sure I have enough time to connect. It’s always a work in progress... What do I hope for as a parent? That they find something they love to do and a great group of friends who love them for who they are."
