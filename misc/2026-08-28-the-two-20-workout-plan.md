Q: Is there a smart way to attempt to even up muscle size and strength. Due to long years of shoeing horses(horrible body position to work in plus long hours working at an anvil with a hammer) I have one leg, arm noticeably smaller than the other. Even upper back muscle definition is not balanced.

[two- workout plan](https://mindpeptide.bbsxyxy.com/)

Answer: Less work on the strong side. Just one set while 3 sets for the weak side. You should also be spending a lot of time each day (15 minutes) doing stickups, band pulls, etc. for upper back.

Q: Can you please give me your best bodyweight excercise superset for working quads and hamstrings?

[The two-](https://science.ketocarbcheck.com/blog/1981915057.html)

Answer: My supersets use non-competing muscle groups, so I don't pair quadricep and hamstring exercises together very often. A good exercise that trains both relatively well is the Bulgarian Split Squat, particularly if you do the 1 and 1/2 rep style.
