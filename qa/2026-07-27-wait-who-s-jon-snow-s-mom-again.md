Fans finally got some clarity in the season-six finale, "The Winds of Winter," in which Bran Stark uses his powers to glimpse the truth: Snow's mother is indeed Lyanna Stark. The pivotal scene shows Snow's birth by Lyanna, the late sister of Ned Stark, who of course then wound up raising the "bastard" Snow.
